# Presentation: 10 minutes + 3 minutes Q&A (9 November 2026)

**Deck:** `Track1_presentation.pptx`. It is built by `scripts/06_presentation.py`, so the numbers always match the paper. Speaker notes are in the deck.
**Visuals:** `figures/slide_*.png` (16:9).
**Roles:** two speakers present (S1: climate, S2: economy); the other three take the Q&A (see `DEFENSE_NOTES.md`).

**Storyline.** We had to choose a data route, and that choice turned out to matter. The data then answer four things in turn:
1. how much it warmed;
2. whether that exceeds the noise (yes for temperature, no for rainfall);
3. when in the year;
4. whether the economy noticed (crops yes, GDP barely).

We close by saying what kind of claim each result is.

## Delivery tips

- **One number per slide.** Say it, then point at it.
- **Name the type of each result** out loud: "this is a description", "this is a correlation". The grading rewards exactly this.
- **Slide 3 is your strongest moment.** It shows the group checked its data, found a problem in its own first version, and fixed it.
- **Stay on time.** Rehearse with a timer: 10 minutes is about 1,300 spoken words.
