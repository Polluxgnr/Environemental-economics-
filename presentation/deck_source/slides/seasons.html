Speaker 1 · 4:50–5:35. Is the warming spread evenly across the year? Mostly, yes. Only in France and Spain is one season clearly ahead: there, summer warmed by about +2.3 degrees against +1.3 in autumn. Germany’s winter warmed even more, +2.5, but German winters vary so much that we cannot call it faster. Summer-heavy warming matters because it lands exactly when crops need water and cities need cooling, which brings us to the economy.
