Pollux · 3:20–4:05. Now all twelve countries, every year since 1940: one row per country, one cell per year, and both panels measured against the same reference, marked on top. On the left, temperature: mostly blue before the 1980s, red after, and every country-year since 2016 is warmer than its 1961–1990 normal. On the right, rainfall: brown is a drier year, blue a wetter one. It flickers, with no colour trend. The years before 1961 are faded: fewer observations stand behind them, so we show them but measure change from 1961. Is the contrast as clear in numbers?
