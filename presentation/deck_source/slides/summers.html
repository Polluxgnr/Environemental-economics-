Augustin · 5:45–6:40. One measurement point first: an annual mean dilutes a heatwave. France's 2003 is 1.2 sigma as a year, but 3.4 sigma as a summer. So we use each country's hot season, its three warmest months. Here is every country's hottest one, and how growth compared with what the country and the year would predict: purple for crops, black for GDP. France 2003: crops −21 points, GDP −1.5. But the big crop losses were heat and drought together: Germany 2003 and Australia 2019 were each country's hottest and driest year, and France 2003 and the US in 1988 were dry too. So, heat or water? And does it reach GDP? We tested both on every country-year.
