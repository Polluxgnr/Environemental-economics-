Speaker 2 · 5:35–6:35. So, the last part of the question: were the unusual climate years unusual for the economy? One measurement point first. An annual mean dilutes a heatwave: France’s 2003 is only 1.2 sigma as a year, but 3.4 sigma as a summer. So we use each country’s hot season, its three warmest months. Here is every country’s hottest one on record, and how growth compared with what the country and the year would predict. Purple is crops, black is GDP. France 2003: crops −21 points, GDP −1.5. In 9 of 12 cases crops fell by more than five points; GDP fell in only 6. These are anecdotes, but they form a pattern, so let us test it.
