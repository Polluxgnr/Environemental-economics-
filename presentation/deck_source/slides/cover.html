Is today’s climate still the one we planned for?

Temperature and rainfall records for 12 countries, 1940–2025, and what they did to the economy

Eliott Beghin · Pollux Gronier · Saty Viard Laroque · Augustin Vlandas

ESSEC BSc AIDAMS · Caterina Seghini · 9 November 2026

Speaker 1 · 0:00–0:35. Every plan we make, a harvest, a dam, a heating system, assumes that a normal year looks like the normal years we remember. Our question is whether that is still true. We read eighty-five years of temperature and rainfall records for twelve countries, measured how far they have moved, compared that with ordinary year-to-year swings, and then asked whether the unusual years were also unusual for the economy. (The stripes behind the title are our data: one row per country, one stripe per year since 1940, blue cooler and red warmer than 1961–1990.)
