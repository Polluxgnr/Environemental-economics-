Twelve countries on four continents, and every one is warmer than in 1961–1990

Country-average temperature, mean 2016–2025 minus 1961–1990 · Copernicus Interactive Climate Atlas, ERA5

Speaker 1 · 0:35–1:15. We chose three countries per continent: temperate Europe, the Mediterranean, the Sahel, East Africa, the monsoon and the Americas, with economies where farming is anywhere from one to over twenty percent of GDP. The headline is already on this map: every one of them is warmer than in 1961 to 1990, from +0.7 degrees in India and Australia to +1.85 in Germany. But before we can say whether that is a lot, we had to answer a more basic question: what exactly is the climate of a country?
