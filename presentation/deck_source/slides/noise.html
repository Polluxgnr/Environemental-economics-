Speaker 1 · 3:50–4:50. Each dot is the change; the grey bands are one and two sigmas of ordinary variation. Temperature is outside two sigma in 11 of 12 countries. Mexico is the clearest case: +1.4 degrees is 5.2 sigma, because its temperature barely varies from year to year. Germany warmed more, but its winters are so variable that it is 2.9 sigma. Australia is the exception, at 1.7. Rainfall is different: in every country the change is smaller than one ordinary year’s swing, so nobody would feel a new climate. But a ten-year average is precise, and in four countries, Brazil, India, Kenya and Senegal, the change in average rainfall is measurable. And it is not where warming is largest: if anything, the fastest warmers have the smallest rainfall changes.
