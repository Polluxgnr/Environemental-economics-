Speaker 1 · 3:50–4:50. Each dot is the change; the grey bands are one and two sigmas of ordinary variation. Temperature is outside two sigma in 11 of 12 countries. Mexico is the clearest case: +1.4 degrees is 5.2 sigma, because its temperature barely varies from year to year. Germany warmed more, but its winters are so variable that it is 2.9 sigma. Australia is the exception, at 1.7. Rainfall: zero of twelve. Kenya and Senegal do show significant drying trends, but the last decade is still within a normal range. And rainfall does not change where warming is largest: the rank correlation across countries is 0.06.
