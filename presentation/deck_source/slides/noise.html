Speaker 2 (Pollux) · 4:05–5:10. It is. Each dot is a country's change; the grey bands are one and two sigma of its ordinary variation. Temperature is beyond two sigma in 11 of 12 countries. Mexico is the clearest case: +1.4 degrees is 5.2 sigma, because its temperature barely varies. Germany warmed more, but its winters swing so much that it is 2.9 sigma. Australia is the one exception, at 1.7. Rainfall is different: everywhere, the change is smaller than one ordinary year's swing, so nobody would feel a new rainfall climate. But a ten-year average is precise, and in four countries, Brazil, India, Kenya and Senegal, the change is measurable: Kenya −14 percent. And it does not follow the warming. So: temperature has clearly moved; rainfall much less. [handover] But when in the year does the warming happen?
