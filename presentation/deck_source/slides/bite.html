Rain moves harvests for a year; heat leaves no robust trace in GDP

+10 % rain: crop output about 2.2 % higher, robust to dropping any country or adding trends, and it lasts one year. Dry years: crops 7.6 points below normal, GDP only 0.27.

Eliott · 7:45–8:35. In harvests rather than GDP, and through water rather than heat. On the right, a year ten percent wetter than normal goes with crop output about 2.2 percent higher, and that is robust: dropping any country, or adding country trends, barely changes it. It lasts one year: the next year output returns to normal. In dry years crop growth is 7.6 points below normal, while GDP growth is only 0.27 below. The likely reason is exposure: in most of our countries agriculture is one to three percent of GDP. That is the IPCC chain from the course: a hazard becomes a loss only through exposure and vulnerability.
