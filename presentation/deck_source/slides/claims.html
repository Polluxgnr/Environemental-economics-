10 · What we can and cannot conclude

Three kinds of claim, three levels of confidence

Description · solid

Every country has warmed

11 of 12 beyond normal variability. Rainfall changed far less (measurable in 4 countries). Summer leads in France and Spain.

Correlation · mixed

Harvests clearly; GDP barely

Dry years go with lower crop output for a year. Warm years show no robust link with GDP growth.

Not claimed

Causal effects of climate change

One hot year allows no adaptation. Needed: many more units, exposure-weighted weather, a channel fixed in advance.

Speaker 4 · 8:35–9:25. What kind of claims are these? The climate results are descriptions, and they are solid. The economic results are correlations. Year-to-year weather is close to random, so our design is close to an experiment; but with twelve countries, rich and poor pooled together, we do not claim causal effects, and an effect limited to poorer, farming economies could be hidden. And a hot year is not climate change: it allows no adaptation and no build-up of damage. Going further would take many more regions, weather weighted by where crops and people are, and a channel fixed in advance.
