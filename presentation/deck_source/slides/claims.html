Speaker 2 · 8:35–9:30. Let us be precise about what kind of claims these are. The climate results are descriptions, and they are solid. The economic results are correlations. Year-to-year weather is close to random, so our design is close to an experiment, but twelve countries give intervals too wide to claim a causal effect on GDP. And a hot year is not climate change: one unusual year allows no adaptation and no build-up of damage. To go further we would need many more units, weather weighted by where crops and people are, and a channel chosen before looking at the data.
